# Editorial Policy (public mirror of /page/editorial-policy)

Persian output style: clear, short, informative, neutral, non-clickbait, minimal
emoji, source-aware; names/numbers/dates preserved exactly. "Reported" never becomes
"confirmed" without evidence. Conflicts are attributed clearly with both sides.
Confirmed and unconfirmed information are visually separated on every story. For
political/war coverage: fact strictly separated from analysis; contested claims
attributed; no engagement-optimized political framing; no persuasion profiles; no
inference of political/religious attributes for commercial targeting.

# Growth

Growth MAY optimize: posting time, platform mix, format, CTA, frequency, topic
volume, card design (Pillow-generated; no Chromium rendering; templates
configurable; no misleading visuals).

Growth MUST NOT alter: facts, evidence, uncertainty, political framing, casualty
information. No fake followers, spam, mass follow/unfollow, automated unsolicited
replies or platform-rule violations. Future commercialization may segment by
explicit interests/topics/non-sensitive categories/voluntary subscriptions — never
inferred sensitive political/religious attributes.

Week-one KPI: 1,000 ACTIVE audience members (target, not guarantee).
